FAQs

Questions, answered

How is a technical audit different from a normal SEO audit?

It goes deeper into crawlability, rendering, log files, indexation and performance — the engineering layer of SEO — rather than content and keywords.

Can you work with our developers?

Yes. We deliver developer-ready tickets with acceptance criteria, review staging, and verify fixes in production.

How long does technical SEO take?

An audit takes days, not months. Implementation depends on the backlog size; critical fixes usually land within the first sprint.

Will fixes hurt our current rankings?

Fixes are staged and monitored; anything risky is tested before release, and migrations include redirect verification.
