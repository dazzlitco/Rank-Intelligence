Frequently Asked Rank Intelligence Questions

What is Rank Intelligence?

Rank Intelligence is an AI-powered search marketing agency offering SEO, PPC, link building, content and website services to businesses worldwide since 2015.

What is the process for working with Rank Intelligence?

It starts with a free proposal: we audit your website, map the strategy to your goals, fix scope and price, then execute with a dedicated project manager and monthly reporting.

What makes Rank Intelligence different from other SEO agencies?

AI-led research with specialist human execution, our proprietary SERPLoft™ platform, transparent reporting and no long lock-in contracts.

What is SEO, and why is it important for my business?

SEO improves your visibility in search results, so customers find you at the moment they are ready to buy — compounding traffic, leads and sales over time.

What are the benefits of hiring an SEO agency?

You get a full specialist team — strategy, technical, content and links — for less than one senior hire, with proven processes and accountability for results.

What is PPC and how does it work?

PPC (pay-per-click) advertising places your business at the top of search results instantly; you pay only when someone clicks, and we optimize campaigns for conversions, not just traffic.

How much does SEO cost?

It depends on scope, competition and goals. Every engagement starts with a free proposal that fixes the price before you commit — see our Plans pages for starting tiers.

How long does SEO take to produce results?

Most clients see measurable movement in 60–90 days, with compounding growth after month three as content, links and technical fixes take effect.

Does Rank Intelligence provide reporting on SEO progress?

Yes — transparent monthly reporting through SERPLoft™ dashboards covering rankings, traffic, leads and the work completed.
