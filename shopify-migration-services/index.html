Shopify Migration Services

Products, customers, orders and — critically — your search rankings, moved to Shopify with a tested plan.

From WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, Wix, Squarespace or custom buildsData, design and search equity moved together — not in separate projectsRedirects tested before launch; rankings monitored after it

Replatforming to Shopify is usually the right call for a growing store: hosting, security, checkout and updates become Shopify's problem, the admin is one a small team can actually run, and the app ecosystem covers most needs without custom code. The risk was never the destination — it is the journey. Shopify's URL structures differ from your current platform's, its data model flattens things your old store kept nested, and a migration run as a pure data export quietly discards years of search equity. This service exists to make the move with everything you built intact — run with the same discipline as our website migration services, specialised for Shopify's particular traps.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Which platforms do you migrate from?

WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, Wix, Squarespace and custom builds.

Will our Google rankings survive?

That is the point of the redirect and structure planning — most stores stabilise quickly when it is done properly.

Can order history move too?

Yes — historical orders and customer accounts are part of the standard data set.

How long does it take?

Typical stores 3–6 weeks depending on catalogue size and customisation; your plan states it.

Will customers have to reset their passwords?

Yes — passwords are encrypted per platform and cannot be transferred by anyone. We handle it gracefully: clear communication, easy reactivation, and incentives where the business wants them. Anyone who promises otherwise is misleading you.

Can we keep our current design?

Usually closely — themes differ in structure, so the rebuild aims for the same look and feel on Shopify's framework, and we flag anything that must adapt before work begins, not after.

What happens to our blog and content pages?

They move too, with redirects — content that earns traffic is part of the store's equity, and Shopify's blog handles standard needs well.

Do you migrate to Shopify Plus as well?

Yes — Plus migrations add checkout extensibility, B2B and multi-store considerations, planned in the same process with the extra surface accounted for.
